Real-World Applications of Robotics

See how the architecture and components you learn are used in actual systems

🏭

Industrial Automation

Articulated robot arms used in welding, painting, assembly, and material handling in automotive and electronics factories.

📦

Warehouse & Logistics

Amazon Kiva robots, AGVs and AMRs that move shelves and packages. Focus on navigation, fleet management, and safety.

🏥

Medical & Surgical Robots

da Vinci Surgical System and other robots that assist surgeons with high-precision minimally invasive procedures.

🚗

Autonomous Vehicles

Self-driving cars use the same sensing → planning → control architecture. LiDAR, cameras, radar + complex decision systems.

🛸

Drones & Aerial Robots

Delivery drones, inspection drones, and agricultural drones. Focus on flight control, GPS, and computer vision.

🧹

Service & Domestic Robots

Vacuum robots (Roomba), delivery robots in hospitals, and hospitality robots. Emphasis on reliability and human interaction.

🌾

Agriculture Robotics

Autonomous tractors, harvesting robots, and crop monitoring drones that increase efficiency and reduce labor.

🤖

Humanoid Robots

Emerging platforms like Tesla Optimus, Figure, and others. Complex balance, bipedal locomotion, and manipulation.

These applications are covered in the course

You will see how the same core architecture and components appear across completely different domains.
